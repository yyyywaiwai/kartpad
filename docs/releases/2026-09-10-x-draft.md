# X draft — September 10 releases

Draft only. No X post was sent. The full version assumes a long-form X post;
use the short post and measurement reply for a compact announcement.

## Full post

New KartPad builds are out for Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Android adds smoother rendering, a refreshed chooser and safer Retro Rewind pack
updates that preserve saves. Controlled local Pixel tests measured at least
15.7% higher FPS in a warm menu and 1.36% in a stationary race scene with the
rendering change enabled. Results vary by scene and device.

iOS/iPadOS adds clearer diagnostic reporting and retains the older-iPad startup
fix. macOS fixes Metal graphics-view ownership during surface recovery.

Driver-specific graphics issues, cup crashes and performance work continue.
Downloads: https://github.com/chrissotraidis/kartpad#downloads

## Short post

New KartPad builds: Android gets rendering improvements, a refreshed chooser and safer Retro pack updates. iOS/iPadOS gets clearer problem reports; Mac fixes Metal view ownership. Downloads: https://github.com/chrissotraidis/kartpad#downloads

## Measurement reply

Local Pixel tests: +15.7% warm-menu FPS, +1.36% in a stationary race scene with the rendering change enabled. These are conservative scene-specific comparisons, not old vs new public APKs or across-device gains. Cup/GPU issues remain.
