# KartPad 0.4.23 for iPhone and iPad

- New racing K icon and refreshed game chooser, with dark mode by default and a saved light/dark toggle.
- Original and Retro Rewind stay easy to switch between; existing setup, help and game options remain available.
- Player Identity detects a missing linked Mii and offers Choose Mii. The repair preserves license progress and friend code, and applies after fully closing and reopening KartPad.

The launcher and identity repair were accepted on a physical iPhone. This release does not claim an Android performance improvement or a new iOS rendering optimization. Update in place to preserve your data. The IPA is unsigned and must be signed for your device; supply your own supported game image.
