**KartPad 0.5.0 is out! 🏁**

Android, iPhone, iPad and Apple Silicon Mac downloads are now together in one release: **Android code135 / Apple build59**.

What's changed:
- **Auto-accelerate is optional.** Turn it off for normal hold-A-to-accelerate controls; your choice is saved.
- Fixed rendering paths that could leave kart thumbnails black or incomplete.
- Fixed reproduced graphics-startup and iPad gameplay crashes, and reduced shader startup pressure.
- Corrected Android controller assignment and duplicate input handling.
- Improved renderer/readback reliability and made ghost-export errors clearer.

Update over your existing installation to preserve game files and saves. The iPhone/iPad IPA needs re-signing with your existing compatible identity. Bring your own supported game data.

Some device-specific graphics problems, performance slowdowns and the older Retro license serial mismatch remain open. Please report whether the new build resolves your existing issue; if it persists, include your build/device and a reviewed log from that attempt.

**Downloads and full notes:** https://github.com/chrissotraidis/kartpad/releases/tag/v0.5.0
